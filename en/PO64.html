Home / Organic Pigments / PO64

Product Overview & Performance

High-chroma halogen-free benzimidazolone orange with excellent heat stability up to 300°C in polyolefins and strong migration resistance in soft PVC.

Recommended Applications

Polyolefins (HDPE/PP), PVC, ABS, PS, high-end packaging inks, powder coatings.

Regulatory & Formulation Notes

Halogen-free (C12H10N6O4); widely used as a diarylide-free & heavy-metal-free orange upgrade.
