About Hwa Shine

Over three decades of dedicated expertise in organic pigments, solvent dyes, and high-performance specialty chemicals from Taipei, Taiwan.

Company Profile

Hwa Shine Co., Ltd. is a specialized colorant and functional chemical supplier serving the plastics, printing inks, industrial coatings, and engineering polymer industries.

With more than 30 years of industry experience, we combine deep formulation know-how with strict supplier qualification to deliver consistent, high-performance organic pigments, solvent dyes, optical brighteners, photoinitiators, and barrier nylon resins (MXD6).

We are strongly committed to regulatory compliance and green chemistry — helping customers transition smoothly to heavy-metal-free, diarylide-free, halogen-free, PFAS-free, and FDA food-contact compliant colorant systems.

Our Core Expertise

01. Organic Pigments & Solvent Dyes

High-chroma, high-transparency, and weather-resistant color solutions tailored for masterbatch, injection molding, automotive OEM coatings, and industrial inks.

02. Eco-Compliant & Regulatory Solutions

Supporting RoHS, REACH, Halogen-Free (IEC), PFAS-Free, and FDA 21 CFR 178.3297 / FCN 2246 food-contact standards with traceable batch quality.

03. Functional Additives & Brighteners

Optical brighteners (OB, OB-1, KCB, KSN, FP-127) and specialty additives that enhance whiteness, brightness, and weatherability across polymers and textiles.

04. Engineering Polymers & UV Curing

High-barrier MXD6 polyamide resins for metal replacement and oxygen-barrier packaging, alongside fast-curing Photoinitiator TPO for UV coatings and inks.
