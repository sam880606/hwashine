Home / Organic Pigments / PR254

Product Overview & Performance

Flagship DPP true red combining 300°C heat resistance, 8-grade lightfastness, 5-grade weatherability, and high opacity/saturation.

Recommended Applications

Automotive OEM & refinish coatings, outdoor industrial paints, engineering plastics, polyolefins.

Regulatory & Formulation Notes

PFAS-free & heavy-metal-free; contains chlorine atoms (not IEC halogen-free).
