Product Overview & Performance

Pigment Red 149 (C.I. 71137, CAS 4948-15-6, Formula C₄₀H₂₆N₂O₄) is a high-performance perylene diimide bright yellowish-red (PV Fast Red B shade) organic pigment renowned for ultra-high tinting strength, 300°C melt stability, 8-grade lightfastness, and 5-grade migration resistance in PVC and polyolefins.

Recommended Applications

PP & PET fine-denier fiber spin dyeing, high-heat engineering plastics (ABS/PC/PP/PVC), halogen-free electronics, and metallic automotive coatings.

Regulatory Highlights

Completely halogen-free (no Cl/Br/F), heavy-metal-free, PFAS-free, and non-azo (no PAA cleavage risk).
