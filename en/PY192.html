Home / Organic Pigments / PY192

Product Overview & Performance

Specialized halogen-free heterocyclic yellow engineered to resist chemical reduction and color shift in high-temperature polyamide (nylon) melts up to 300°C.

Recommended Applications

Nylon (PA6 / PA66 / glass-filled PA), PBT, PET, high-temperature engineering plastics.

Regulatory & Formulation Notes

Halogen-free (C19H10N4O2) & PFAS-free.
