Pigment Yellow 191 (PY191)

Pyrazolone Monoazo Calcium Lake · Reddish Yellow · Heat Resistance 300°C · Lightfastness 6–7

Home / Organic Pigments / PY191

Product Overview & Performance

Cost-effective 300°C high-heat reddish-yellow for injection-molded plastics with low warpage in HDPE. Avoid prolonged acidic/aqueous outdoor exposure.

Recommended Applications

High-temperature polyolefins (HDPE/PP), ABS, PS, PC, economical high-heat plastics.

Regulatory & Formulation Notes

Contains chlorine & calcium salt; PFAS-free and heavy-metal-free.
