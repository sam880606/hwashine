Home / Organic Pigments / PO13

Product Overview & Performance

Economical, high-tinting-strength bright yellowish-orange pigment widely used in indoor packaging inks and rubber compounds.

Recommended Applications

Flexo & gravure packaging inks, rubber coloration, indoor plastics, water-based textile printing.

Regulatory & Formulation Notes

Diarylide/chlorinated structure; not recommended for outdoor weatherability or >200°C processing.
