Home / Organic Pigments / PB60

Product Overview & Performance

Top-tier polycyclic reddish-blue pigment with exceptional weatherability even in pale TiO2 tints and metallic finishes, low warpage in HDPE, and 5-grade PVC migration resistance.

Recommended Applications

Automotive OEM metallic coatings, coil coatings, low-warpage HDPE, engineering plastics, security inks.

Regulatory & Formulation Notes

Completely copper-free and halogen-free molecular formula (C28H14N2O4).
