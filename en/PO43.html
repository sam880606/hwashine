Product Overview & Performance

High-performance perinone orange (Vat Orange 7) offering exceptional lightfastness, weatherability, solvent resistance, and high thermal stability.

Recommended Applications

High-end outdoor coatings, engineering plastics, spin dyeing, high-weatherability PVC.

Regulatory & Formulation Notes

Heavy-metal-free; verify batch specs for specific food-contact or halogen regulations.
