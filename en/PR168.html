Home / Organic Pigments / PR168

Product Overview & Performance

One of the most weather-resistant organic scarlet pigments available (8-grade lightfastness even in pale 1:10 TiO2 tints) with superb overspray and solvent fastness.

Recommended Applications

Automotive OEM metallic paints, fluorocarbon/coil architectural coatings, lead-free PR104 replacement, outdoor PVC.

Regulatory & Formulation Notes

RoHS compliant (not PBB/PBDE), but contains 2 covalent bromine atoms (C22H8Br2O2) — not IEC halogen-free.
