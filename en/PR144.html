Product Overview & Performance

Pigment Red 144 (C.I. 20735, CAS 5280-78-4, Formula C₄₀H₂₃Cl₅N₆O₄) is a classic high-molecular-weight disazo condensation red offering 300°C heat resistance, 5-grade migration fastness in soft PVC, strong tinting power, and high cost-effectiveness for plastics and fibers.

Recommended Applications

PP/HDPE/LDPE/ABS/PS masterbatch & molding, PP fiber spin dyeing, soft PVC cables & leather, and high-grade packaging inks.

Regulatory Notes

Heavy-metal-free, RoHS compliant & PFAS-free. Contains 5 chlorine atoms (not IEC halogen-free — choose PR149 for halogen-free).
